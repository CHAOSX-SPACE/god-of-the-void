Microsoft PowerPoint (tested 2026-11-11).
Each block is sent as ["applescript", "<the block>"], with plain quotes (no backslashes \).

PRESENTATION WITH SLIDES:
tell application "Microsoft PowerPoint"
activate
make new presentation
set s to make new slide at end of active presentation with properties {layout:slide layout title slide}
set content of text range of text frame of shape 1 of s to "Title"
set content of text range of text frame of shape 2 of s to "Subtitle"
set s2 to make new slide at end of active presentation with properties {layout:slide layout text slide}
set content of text range of text frame of shape 1 of s2 to "Points"
set content of text range of text frame of shape 2 of s2 to "One\nTwo\nThree"
end tell

COUNT: tell application "Microsoft PowerPoint" to count slides of active presentation

SAVE (.pptx or .pdf; inside the project) — with your own path PowerPoint does NOT save and does NOT warn:
["guardar", "Microsoft PowerPoint", "presentation.pptx"]
OPEN: ["abrir", "Microsoft PowerPoint", "/ABSOLUTE/path/p.pptx"]
CLOSE without saving: tell application "Microsoft PowerPoint" to close active presentation saving no
